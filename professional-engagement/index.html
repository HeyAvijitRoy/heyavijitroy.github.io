Community leadership & technical outreach

Mapping Bangladesh

Local knowledge, shared maps

Google Map Maker Regional Expert Reviewer · Mapping Bangladesh executive member 2012–2015 · Bangladesh

As a Regional Expert Reviewer for Google Map Maker and an executive member of Mapping Bangladesh, I organized mapping events and taught contributors how to add and review local geographic information. MapUps brought community members together to improve the representation of familiar places on Google Maps. Contemporary event records identify me as Avijit Roy Kabyo.

  • Organizing: hosted MapUps at North South University and BRAC University, and the Experts’ MapUp in Dhaka in 2013. Google sponsored the NSU event.
  • Teaching & speaking: served as an invited speaker and mapping instructor at Bangladesh’s first Google MapUp in Rangpur (November 23, 2012) and delivered invited talks, including “Making Maps for your Project, Quickly & Easily” at GBG Dhaka’s 11th Meetup on May 21, 2013.
  • Public outreach: discussed Google Maps and the Mapping Bangladesh community on ATN News’ Young Nite.

The emphasis on local knowledge connects with my current work on representation in digital systems, including research on underserved language communities.

Selected delegation & policy dialogue

Student Conference on U.S. Affairs

SCUSA 71 · Climate Change roundtable

U.S. Military Academy, West Point, New York October 30–November 2, 2019

Selected as a delegate through One To World, I participated in the Climate Change roundtable at the 71st Student Conference on U.S. Affairs. The conference brought civilian student delegates and West Point cadets together to examine domestic and foreign policy challenges.

I contributed to drafting and presenting the roundtable’s policy recommendations, working with fellow delegates to translate discussion of climate change into a shared policy proposal.
