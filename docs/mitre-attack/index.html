02 / Page anatomy

Know where to look on a technique page

This map follows the current Enterprise page for T1566.002, Spearphishing Link. Choose a section to see the question it answers.

TA…Tactic ID: the goal.
T…Technique ID: the behavior.
T….002Subtechnique ID: a specific variant.
DET… / AN…Detection strategy / analytic. These are separate defensive entries.

Procedure ≠ subtechnique

A subtechnique is a named category of behavior. A procedure example describes a reported implementation by a group or software. Its row may link to a group (G…) or software (S…).

03 / Navigate on MITRE

Find a new behavior without knowing its ID

Open the official site alongside this guide. Choose the route that matches the clue you have; both routes end with checking the entry itself.

You know the adversary's goal

  1. Open the Enterprise Matrix ↗ and find the tactic column that describes the goal.
  2. Open that tactic, then scan its listed techniques. Select a candidate based on the observed action.
  3. On the technique page, check its description and any listed subtechniques. Follow a subtechnique only when the evidence supports that narrower behavior.

You know the observed behavior

  1. Use the search control on MITRE ATT&CK ↗. Search for the action in plain words, such as operating system version.
  2. Look for a technique or subtechnique result in the correct domain. Open it; a matching title alone is not enough.
  3. Read the description, tactic, platforms, and nearby entries. If the match is weak, revise your search words or browse the matrix.

Tactic cheat sheet: match the goal

Every Enterprise tactic answers “The adversary is trying to…”. Finish that sentence from your evidence, then open the matching column. Columns are listed left to right as they appear on the matrix, but real intrusions do not have to follow this order.

  1. TA0043Reconnaissancegather information to plan future operations
  2. TA0042Resource Developmentestablish resources to support operations
  3. TA0001Initial Accessget into your network
  4. TA0002Executionrun malicious code
  5. TA0003Persistencemaintain their foothold
  6. TA0004Privilege Escalationgain higher-level permissions
  7. TA0005Defense Evasionavoid being detected
  8. TA0006Credential Accesssteal account names and passwords
  9. TA0007Discoveryfigure out your environment
  10. TA0008Lateral Movementmove through your environment
  11. TA0009Collectiongather data of interest to their goal
  12. TA0011Command and Controlcommunicate with compromised systems to control them
  13. TA0010Exfiltrationsteal data
  14. TA0040Impactmanipulate, interrupt, or destroy systems and data

Notice the IDs are not in column order (TA0011 sits before TA0010). An ID is a stable name, not a position.

Before you record an ID

The tactic explains the goal; the technique describes the action. A procedure example illustrates reported use. A group or software name in that section does not identify who caused your event. Record what you observed separately from what you infer.

Try it yourself: a different behavior

Hypothetical clue: After gaining access to a Windows computer, an adversary checks its operating system version and hardware details. Find the closest Enterprise ATT&CK technique using either route above.

Write down before revealing the answer:
  • The tactic and technique IDs, names, and official URL.
  • The words in the clue that match the technique description.
  • Whether MITRE lists a more specific subtechnique.
  • One thing the clue does not establish.
Show a worked answer

TA0007, Discovery ↗ is the goal. T1082, System Information Discovery ↗ describes learning about the operating system and hardware. The page lists no subtechniques. The clue does not identify an adversary group, or prove what the adversary did next. If the evidence instead concerns locating files and folders, compare T1083, File and Directory Discovery ↗.

Record your mapping

A mapping is only as good as the note behind it. Keep what you saw separate from what you concluded, so someone else can check your work.

Observed evidence:  (what the log, alert, or report actually shows)
Tactic:             TA____ · ________  (the goal)
Technique:          T____ · ________   (the action)
Subtechnique:       T____.___ or "none / not supported by evidence"
Official URL:       https://attack.mitre.org/...
ATT&CK version:     v__ (from the page's Version Permalink)
Confidence:         high / medium / low, and why
Not established:    (e.g. who did it, whether it succeeded, what happened next)

05 / Practice

Can you find the right part of the page?

Seven short questions: some ask where to look on a page, others give you a new clue to map. After each answer, open the linked MITRE entry and check it yourself.

A reliable reading order

Choose the domain → name the goal → identify the behavior → check the most specific subtechnique → read procedure examples and detection strategy → record the ID and official URL. A technique may belong to more than one tactic, so verify the context.
